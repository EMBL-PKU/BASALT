# Extra binners

The maintained instructions for MetaBinner, VAMB, and LorBin are in the [BASALT extra-binner guide](BASALT_Guide/docs/extra-binners.md).

The guide documents adapter dependencies, failure semantics, validation, provenance, and citation requirements. In particular, an optional-binner failure can be logged while the main CheckM2 workflow continues; verify which candidate sets actually contributed to the result.
